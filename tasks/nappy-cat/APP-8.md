# APP-8: Bring the cat companion to life

**Type:** Task

**Stage:** 5. First extras

**Priority:** Normal

**Status:** Planned

**Dependencies:** [APP-3](../nappy-cat/APP-3.md)

## Goal

Create the first small animated cat that reflects focus, breaks, idle time, and celebration without overwhelming the productivity app.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A small initial set of states responds coherently to real app events and handles rapid or repeated transitions.
- [ ] Assets are original or appropriately licensed and their size and memory cost are recorded.
- [ ] Reduced motion is supported; hidden or idle presentation avoids unnecessary animation and resource use.
- [ ] The initial appearance is readable and usable; an extensive skin catalogue and every animation are outside this first pass.

## Scope and sequencing

Start after the YouTube Music stage outcome is accepted.
